What is WASHways?

A growing set of data tools for people who plan, fund and run water, sanitation and hygiene services: governments, NGOs, utilities and researchers.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources such as the Global Burden of Disease, DHS surveys, OECD aid data and satellite imagery, plus field monitoring in Malawi. Each tool explains its sources.

Ideas or problems?

Suggest a tool, report a bug or ask about using the tools in your work. Get in touch.
