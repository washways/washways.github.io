# Privacy and cookies

WASHways tools are free and need no account, apart from a few monitoring tools that partners sign in to.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how many people use each tool, which countries they come from, and how they found us. This helps us decide what to improve and build next. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

- **If you accept cookies**, Google Analytics sets cookies that let us count returning visitors.
- **If you decline**, no analytics cookies are set. Google receives only basic, cookieless signals (for example, that a page was viewed) that cannot be linked to you.
- Advertising and personalisation features are always switched off.

Your choice applies to washways.org and to the tools at apps.washways.org, and is remembered for a year. You can change it at any time: <button type="button" class="chip" data-ww-consent>Cookie settings</button>

## Contact form

If you send a message, we use your name and email only to reply to you.
